Compare scheduling methods

Both methods use the same students, rooms and available sessions.

Both methods try to find a useful timetable quickly. Neither guarantees the fewest sessions. If one leaves exams unscheduled, another ordering may work. Runtime is a single browser measurement.

DISCRETE MATHEMATICS IN THIS PROJECT

Sets and relations

Students and subjects form sets. Enrolment links each student to the subjects they take.

Graphs and coloring

Subjects are vertices. Shared students create edges. Different colors keep conflicting subjects in different sessions.

Bipartite matching

Exams are matched to available rooms with enough seats. Each room holds one exam per session.
